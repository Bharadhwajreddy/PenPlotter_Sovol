- **[F]** = sourced fact (a government, regulator or primary document, read directly where possible)
- **[S]** = secondary source (press, blogs, aggregators); check it before quoting externally
- **[U]** = unverified or conflicting; do not quote without checking
- **[A]** = our assumption (used only in the sizing model)

- **Unit = one "launched business"**: a first-time or early business that raises ₹50k–₹10 lakh (and/or gets guidance) through Fish Tank and opens.
- **Revenue per launch = ₹33,500; contribution = ₹14,000 [A, illustrative, given by the brief].**
- **Demand proxy [A]:** first-time ("new entrepreneur") MUDRA loans in the Kishor + Tarun bands (₹50k–₹10L). This is the best official count of first-timers who borrow in exactly Fish Tank's band. It *understates* demand: it leaves out people refused credit (the SIDBI gap) and franchisees who use other loans. It *overstates* what Fish Tank can reach: many of these borrowers are happy with bank MUDRA.

Capital Fish Tank must route through the P2P partner, at the TS average ticket of ₹4.17 L [A]:
- Y3 conservative: about ₹29 cr a year
- Y3 optimistic: about ₹125 cr a year, which is **≈8% of the entire Indian P2P industry's current AUM (~₹1,500 cr)**

At an assumed ₹1 lakh a year per backer [A], that means about 2,900 or about 12,500 active backers.

Break-even check [A]: with ₹14,000 contribution per launch, every ₹1 crore of fixed cost (team, tech, compliance, field verification) needs about **715 launches a year**. A lean ₹3 cr a year cost base needs about 2,150 launches a year. The conservative case does not reach that in 3 years; the optimistic case passes it in Y3.

### 6.4 Sensitivities worth knowing

- If Fish Tank only did franchises, the two-state Tier-2/3 pool is about 1,000–2,500 launches a year (₹3–8 cr revenue pool). That is too small to break even, so franchise works as an entry point but the product must cover non-franchise first-timers quickly.
- Credit losses: Telangana Kishor NPA is 9.4% and Tarun 6.3% [F]; P2P 90+ DPD is about 20% [S]. Backers carry this risk under RBI rules. A visible loss rate near 10% will hurt backer retention more than any shortage of demand.
- Possible P2P caps (₹10 L per borrower, ₹50k per lender per borrower [U]) match the ticket band, but they force crowd-style syndication of each pitch.

## 7. Verdict: is the market big enough?

**Yes on demand, conditional on supply and execution.**

**Why yes**
1. India produces about **30–47 lakh first-time borrowers a year in exactly the ₹50k–₹10L band** (MUDRA Kishor + Tarun, first-timers). That is a ₹0.9–2.0 lakh crore yearly capital need and a ₹10,000–15,700 crore revenue pool at ₹33,500 per launch.
2. Underneath that sits a documented ₹30 lakh crore MSME credit gap, which is worse for micro (25%), trading (33%) and women-owned (35%) businesses.
3. Telangana and AP alone produce about **1.8 lakh in-band first-timers a year** (Telangana 87,920 is a hard SLBC figure). Even 1–3% of that is 700–3,000 launches a year.
4. Culturally, both sides exist: Shark Tank drew 62,000 applications in season 1. India has 13 crore equity investors, 10 crore SIP accounts and a deep chit-fund habit in TS/AP.

**Why it is conditional**
1. **The beachhead is small.** Franchise launches in TS+AP Tier-2/3 come to roughly 1,000–2,500 a year at best (assumption-heavy). A single Tier-2 city has only about 1,900 formal in-band first-time loans a year. Year-1 volume will be tens to low hundreds, not thousands.
2. **The money pipe is narrow and stressed.** The whole NBFC-P2P industry holds about ₹1,500 crore AUM with about 20% of loans 90+ days overdue. MUDRA Kishor NPAs run about 9–12%. Fish Tank's limit is backer capital and loss rates, not entrepreneurs.
3. **Guidance does not pay well on its own** (ffreedom: about ₹35 cr revenue on about 1 crore users). Franchisor and lender fees have to carry the revenue.
4. **Unit economics need scale.** At ₹14,000 contribution per launch, break-even needs roughly 700 launches per ₹1 cr of fixed cost. Only the optimistic path gets there within 3 years.

**Bottom line:** the market is large enough to build a meaningful business: a ₹240–360 cr a year serviceable pool in TS+AP alone and ₹10,000+ cr nationally. The franchise-only wedge in one Tier-2 city is not. Plan to:
- (a) add non-franchise first-timers (shops, food, services) within the first year;
- (b) expand to about 6 Tier-2 cities, and probably Hyderabad, by year 3;
- (c) treat backer acquisition and loss control as the main constraint;
- (d) confirm the RBI P2P borrower and lender caps before fixing the ticket range.
