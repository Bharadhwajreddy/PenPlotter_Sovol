# Fish Tank: clickable v1

A one-file prototype of Fish Tank, an app where people pitch a business by video and get backers, mentors and help. It runs in any browser with no build step. All people and numbers in it are sample data, and no real money moves.

## Run it

Open `index.html` in a browser.

## Put it online

- **Vercel:** import this repository, set the root directory to `fishtank-app`, and deploy with no framework preset.
- **Netlify or GitHub Pages:** serve the `fishtank-app` folder as a static site.

## What's inside

- Role picker: start a business, build a startup, grow a business, back businesses, mentor, partner, or just explore. Each role gets its own short setup and next-steps plan.
- The Tank: a pitch feed with filters, pitch pages with the Ask Card, Proof Stack and Fish Score, and an "offer to back" flow.
- Pitch builder: five steps with a live preview that shows backers the yearly rate of each deal.
- Learn: proven business formats, a government scheme finder (MUDRA, PMEGP, Stand-Up India, Startup India Seed Fund, CGTMSE) and short guides.
- Mentors with call booking, an account page with demo verification, and safety rules with a report form.
- "Why" (demand study with sources) and "Model" (business model) pages for presenting.
- Menus and setup in English, Hindi and Telugu.
